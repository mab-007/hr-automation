Why Wheelz365

Built for speed. Payroll that moves
as fast as your team hires.

Stop doing the things that don't scale. Wheelz365 handles onboarding, attendance, statutory filings and the monthly run so your HR team can focus on the people side.

Offer letter, employee record, KYC, bank verification, PF and ESI registration and payroll enrolment fire off the moment a candidate signs. No forms to re-key.

Biometric punches, shift rosters, holidays and approved leave flow straight into the salary calculation. Loss of pay and overtime are computed as the month runs, not after it.

Lock the cycle and Wheelz365 computes gross, deductions and net pay, generates the bank payout file, issues payslips and posts the journal to your books in a single run.

PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS schedules are prepared with the run and tracked to the filing date, with a full audit trail of who approved what and when.

Payslips, tax declarations, leave balances and reimbursement claims sit with the employee on WhatsApp and the portal — so HR stops being a ticket queue.
